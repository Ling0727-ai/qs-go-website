Features

Everything you need for AI upscaling

QualityScaler packs a complete AI super-resolution pipeline into a clean desktop app.

Image Upscaling

2×, 3×, 4× upscaling with Real-ESRGAN, BSRGAN, Real-CUGAN, and more. Tiled inference prevents GPU OOM on large images.

Video Upscaling

Frame-by-frame AI processing with FFmpeg. Preserves original audio, framerate, and VFR (variable frame rate) streams.

Face Enhancement

Cascaded pipeline: detect faces at native resolution, enhance with CodeFormer, then seamlessly blend back via alpha feathering.

Auto Backend Selection

TensorRT → ONNX CUDA → ONNX CPU → fallback interpolation. Automatically picks the fastest available backend for your hardware.

Batch Processing

Drop an entire folder of images and let the async goroutine pipeline process them all. Feeder → inference → saver with zero GPU starvation.

FP16 Quantization

Half-precision inference delivers 2–3× throughput improvement with negligible quality loss. Zero-copy float buffer reuse via sync.Pool.

How It Works

Three steps to higher resolution

No cloud uploads. Everything runs locally on your machine.

1

Drop your files

Drag & drop images or video files into QualityScaler. Batch folders supported.

2

Choose a model

Pick from 10+ AI models — Real-ESRGAN for general use, CodeFormer for faces, or anime-optimized variants.

3

Export & enjoy

Your upscaled output is saved locally. No watermark, no file size limit, no subscription.
