Features

Everything you need for AI upscaling

QualityScaler packs a complete AI super-resolution pipeline into a clean desktop app.

Image Upscaling

2×, 3×, 4× upscaling with Real-ESRGAN, BSRGAN, Real-CUGAN, and more.

Video Upscaling

Frame-by-frame AI processing with FFmpeg. Preserves original audio, framerate, and VFR streams.

Face Enhancement

Cascaded pipeline: detect faces, enhance with CodeFormer, then blend back via alpha feathering.

Auto Backend Selection

TensorRT → ONNX CUDA → ONNX CPU → fallback. Auto-picks the fastest backend.

Batch Processing

Drop a folder and let the async pipeline process everything. Feeder → inference → saver with zero GPU starvation.

FP16 Quantization

Half-precision inference delivers 2–3× throughput with negligible quality loss.

How It Works

Three steps to higher resolution

No cloud uploads. Everything runs locally on your machine.

1

Drop your files

Drag & drop images or video files into QualityScaler. Batch folders supported.

2

Choose a model

Pick from 10+ AI models — Real-ESRGAN, CodeFormer, anime-optimized variants, and more.

3

Export & enjoy

Your upscaled output is saved locally. No watermark, no file size limit, no subscription.
